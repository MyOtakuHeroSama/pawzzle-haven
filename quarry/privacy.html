1. Who we are

This policy applies to the Rumble Quarry mobile game for Android (package name com.rumblequarry.game), published on Google Play by the Rumble Quarry team ("we", "us"). We are responsible (the "data controller") for the data processed through the game as described here.

Contact: otakuherosama.dev@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

a) Gameplay statistics: Unity Analytics (only if you allow it)

When you choose Accept on the first screen (or turn on Gameplay stats in the settings), the game sends:

  • A random analytics ID created on your device. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account, and a new one is created if you ask us to delete your data.
  • Game events: sessions (start, end, play time), depth reached and rock layers unlocked, boss boulders broken, geodes opened (rarity and which relic was found, collection size), upgrades at milestone levels, moving to a new quarry, offline earnings collected, tutorial progress, rewarded ad rewards claimed, and ad impressions (ad type, whether it was watched to the end, watch time, whether it was clicked).
  • Device information: device manufacturer and model, Android version, processor type and core count, total memory, screen size and density, graphics chip, battery level, device language, and app version.
  • Approximate country, which Unity derives from your IP address. We do not collect precise location.

b) Advertising: Unity Ads

To show ads, the Unity Ads software in the game collects your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Ads are personalised only if you allow it. If you choose Essential only or turn off Personalised ads, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests. Unity still uses limited data to deliver ads, cap how often you see them, and prevent fraud.

c) Data that stays on your device

Your game progress (coins, gems, upgrades, quarry depth, collected relics, deeds), settings (sound, music, vibration, language) and privacy choices are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us and are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

d) What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, or payment information. The game has no account system, no chat and no in-app purchases.

3. How we use data

  • To balance the game economy and geode odds, improve the tutorial, and fix problems (analytics).
  • To understand how long and how often the game is played, and on which devices and in which countries, so we can support them.
  • To show ads that keep the game free, and to personalise them if you allowed it.
  • To measure ad performance and prevent fraud and abuse.

We do not make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you based solely on automated processing.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Turkey and similar laws)

  • Consent: gameplay analytics and personalised advertising. You can withdraw consent at any time in the settings; this does not affect processing before withdrawal.
  • Legitimate interests: showing non-personalised ads to fund a free game, ad fraud prevention, and keeping the game secure and working.

The app store you install the game from (Google Play) processes your account, download and payment data under its own privacy policy. We do not receive that data, apart from aggregated download and crash statistics the store shows to developers.

6. International transfers

Unity may process data on servers in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers are protected by safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. For users in Turkey, transfers abroad are made under Article 9 of the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) on the basis of your consent or the safeguards it provides for.

7. How long we keep data

  • Analytics data is kept only as long as needed to improve the game, and is deleted when you request deletion.
  • Unity Ads keeps advertising data according to Unity's own privacy policy.
  • Data on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

8. Your choices and rights

  • In the game: Settings ⚙️ → Privacy settings lets you turn personalised ads and gameplay stats on or off at any time.
  • Delete your data: Settings ⚙️ → Privacy settings → Delete my data sends a deletion request to Unity Analytics for your analytics ID and stops collection. You can also email us.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads or Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and not be discriminated against for using these rights.
  • How to ask: email otakuherosama.dev@gmail.com. Because we do not know who you are, please include the in-game deletion confirmation or a description of your device so we can help. We reply within 30 days.
  • Complaints: you can contact your local data protection authority (in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Authority, kvkk.gov.tr).

9. Children

Rumble Quarry is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. In some countries the age for consenting to data processing is higher (up to 16 in the EEA); younger players should ask a parent before accepting. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data sent by the game is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We keep the data we handle to a minimum, and we do not have accounts or passwords that could be leaked. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we and our providers take reasonable measures to protect your data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game gets new features. The new version is published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the game.
